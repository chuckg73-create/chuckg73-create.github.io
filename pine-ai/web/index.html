How it works

1

Tell Cedar the goal

"Lower my internet bill" or "Cancel my gym membership." Add any account details.

2

Cedar plans the call

It researches the company, drafts a strategy, and prepares for pushback before dialing.

3

It makes the call

Cedar speaks and listens in real time, adapting to whatever the rep says.

4

You get the result

A summary of what happened, what changed, and any confirmation numbers.

Under the hood: Claude plans and drives every conversation · real-time speech-to-text and text-to-speech give it a voice · Twilio places the actual calls. Read the architecture →
