How does it know what's in my kitchen?

Snap a photo of your fridge or pantry and KindredTable identifies the ingredients. You can always review the list and add or remove anything before cooking.

How do I get recipes matched to my taste?

Open your profile (top-left) and set the foods and cuisines you love, anything to avoid, your spice level, skill, and time. Recipes are ranked to fit you.

I'm vegan / have allergies — is that respected?

Yes. Set your diet and allergens in your profile. Every recipe strictly excludes anything that breaks those rules, in both the daily ideas and craving search.

The read-aloud voice sounds robotic — can I improve it?

KindredTable automatically uses the best voice installed on your device. For a much more natural voice, download an “Enhanced” English voice in Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Spoken Content ▸ Voices, and the app will use it.

Voice commands or Siri aren't working.

Cook Mode needs microphone and speech-recognition permission (allow them when prompted, or in Settings). For Siri, say “next step in KindredTable.” Voice features require a physical device.

Do I need an account?

No accounts, ever. Your taste profile stays on your device, and sharing with family happens directly by QR code.
